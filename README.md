# Computer Forensics

Computer forensics is the process of identifying, preserving, analyzing and presenting digital evidence from computers and storage devices. It helps investigators reconstruct events, determine what actions occurred on a system, identify who performed those actions and also establish timelines that can support investigations.

In this repository, I document my journey through computer forensics, including practical investigations, forensic methodologies, tools, artifacts, and case studies. The focus is not only on understanding where digital evidence exists, but also on learning how to interpret that evidence accurately and professionally.

---

## Why Computer Forensics Matters

Just so you know, every interaction with a computer leaves traces behind. From opening a file, plugging in a USB drive, browsing the web, installing softwares or running a program  leaves evidences behind. And those evidences can stick around long after the activity is over.

Computer forensics enables investigators to:

- Reconstruct user activities that is past or present
- Identify unauthorized actions
- Recover deleted evidence
- Establish timelines of events
- Verify or refute claims
- Support legal, corporate and incident response investigations

Understanding these artifacts helps transform raw data into meaningful evidence that can withstand scrutiny.
